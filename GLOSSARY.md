# Hushfield

One evolving ambient piece, shaped through related sound parts and shared finishing controls.

## Language

**Piece**:
The complete evolving composition, including its Foundation, Texture, Motion and shared sound settings.

**Foundation**:
The ambient voices that establish a Piece's harmonic context and supply its live Texture material.

**Texture**:
A granular sound part made from recent Foundation audio, a frozen phrase or optional imported material.

**Motion**:
The Orbit-driven sound part, following the Foundation's harmony unless explicitly unlinked.

**Blend**:
The relative audible levels of Foundation, Texture and Motion.

**Solo**:
A temporary listening state that isolates one part without changing the Blend.

**Frozen phrase**:
A fixed excerpt of Foundation audio used as Texture material; it retains the harmony present when captured.
