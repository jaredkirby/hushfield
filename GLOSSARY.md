# Hushfield

One evolving ambient piece, shaped through related sound parts and shared finishing controls.

## Language

### The Piece and its parts

**Piece**:
The complete evolving composition, including its Foundation, Texture, Motion and shared sound settings.
_Avoid_: Track, song, editor

**Foundation**:
The ambient voices that establish a Piece's harmonic context and supply its live Texture material.

**Texture**:
A granular sound part made from recent Foundation audio, a Frozen phrase or optional source material.
_Avoid_: Sample, when referring to the sound part rather than its material

**Motion**:
The Orbit-driven sound part, following Foundation's harmony unless explicitly unlinked.
_Avoid_: Orbit, when referring to the sound part rather than its note-generating motion

**Orbit**:
The rotating shape, bouncing balls and trigger marks whose movement generates Motion notes.

**Blend**:
The relative audible levels of Foundation, Texture and Motion.

**Solo**:
A temporary listening state that isolates one part without changing the Blend.

### Material and musical development

**Frozen phrase**:
A fixed excerpt of Foundation audio used as Texture material; it retains the harmony present when captured.
_Avoid_: Hold chord, Hold picture

**Harmony link**:
The relationship that tunes new Motion notes to Foundation's current chord tones; it does not retune Texture material.

**Phrase**:
The repeating musical arc that changes the activity and relative presence of the three sound parts over time.
_Avoid_: Frozen phrase, when referring to the evolving arc rather than captured material

**Scene**:
A named Foundation configuration, distinct from the settings of the complete Piece.
_Avoid_: Whole piece preset

**Whole piece preset**:
A named starting configuration for all three sound parts, their relationships, patches and shared finishing.
_Avoid_: Scene, patch

**Sound patch**:
The voice and effect settings for a Motion trigger or ball, including settings shared by its bank.
_Avoid_: Whole piece preset

### Finishing and viewing

**Space**:
The shared filter, stereo width and room that finish the three sound parts together.

**Depth**:
The per-part room sends and room filtering that place the sound parts at different distances within Space.

**Tape**:
The shared tape coloration and echo applied to the complete Piece.

**Hold chord**:
A state that keeps Foundation's current harmony while the Piece continues to develop.
_Avoid_: Freeze phrase, Hold picture

**Hold picture**:
A state that keeps Visual's current image while the Piece continues to play.
_Avoid_: Hold chord, Freeze phrase
